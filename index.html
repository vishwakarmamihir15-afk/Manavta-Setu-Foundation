Jahan Dard Hai Wahan Sahara, Aur Jahan Bhookh Hai Wahan Samman

Kai baar ek waqt ki garam roti, sar par ek chhat, ya waqt par mili dawa kisi poore parivar ki zindagi bacha leti hai. Manavta Setu Foundation ka uddeshya un logon tak pahunchna hai jinke paas kehne ko toh koi nahi hota, par unki aankhon me ummeed abhi bhi zinda hoti hai.

Abhi Daan Karein Hamare Seva Karya Dekhein

Hamara Vishwas: Madad sirf daan nahi hoti—madad ek rishta hai. Jab hum kisi bhookhe ko khana khilate hain, kisi beghar ko chhat dete hain, ya kisi beemar bachche ke ilaaj me saath khade hote hain, tab insaniyat ka asli setu banta hai.

Hamare Mukhya Seva Karya

Har Zaroorat Ke Waqt Ek Majboot Kandha

Manavta Setu Foundation zameeni star par un buniyadi zarooraton ke liye kaam karta hai jinke bina kisi bhi insaan ka jeevan adhoora aur asurakshit rehta hai.

🍲

Annadaan — Koi Bhookha Na Soye

Sadak kinare rehne wale majdooron, bujurgon aur zarooratmand parivaron tak shuddh, garam aur paushtik bhojan pahunchana. Ek thaali khana kisi ke chehre par sukoon aur jeene ki taqat laata hai.

Niyamit Bhojan & Ration Sahayata
🏠

Aashray — Beghar Parivaron Ko Chhat

Jinke paas apna ghar nahi hai ya jo khule aasman ke neeche sardi, garmi aur barish jhelne ko majboor hain, unke liye surakshit rehne ki vyavastha, kambal, aur buniyadi suvidhaon ka prabandh.

Surakshit Niwas & Parivar Punarvas
🌊

Aapda Rahat — Baadh Aur Sankat Me Saath

Jab kabhi baadh, toofan ya kisi prakritik aapda se logon ke ghar aur rozgar ujad jaate hain, tab turant sukha ration, peene ka saaf paani, kapde aur zaroori rahati samagri pahunchana.

Turant Emergency Relief Team
🩺

Bal Chikitsa — Beemar Bachchon Ka Ilaaj

Paise ki kami ke kaaran kisi masoom bachche ka ilaaj nahi rukna chahiye. Zarooratmand bachchon ki dawai, doctor consultation, jaanch aur hospital sahayata me seedha yogdan.

Dawai, Jaanch & Hospital Sahayog
📚

Bal Shiksha — Kal Ka Ujjwal Bhavishya

Garib aur vanchit parivaron ke bachchon ko kitabein, copy-pen, school bag aur padhai ke liye protsahan dena, taaki woh apne parivar ko garibi se bahar nikaal sakein.

Stationery & Shiksha Protsahan
🤝

Bujurg & Mahila Samman Seva

Akeli mahilaon aur besahara bujurgon ke liye dawai, kapde, aur samajik samman ke saath unhe yeh ehsaas dilana ki woh is samaj me akele nahi hain.

Samvedansheel Zameeni Sahayata
Hamara Sankalp

Kyunki Insaniyat Ka Asli Matlab Tab Samajh Aata Hai Jab Hum Kisi Ke Aansoo Ponchhte Hain

Hamare aas-paas aise hazaron log hain jo subah is chinta me uthte hain ki aaj shaam ka khana kahan se aayega, ya barish aane par apne bachchon ko kahan chhupayenge. Kisi maa ke paas apne beemar bachche ki dawai kharidne ke paise nahi hote, toh kisi baadh-peedit parivar ke paas sirf yaadein bach jaati hain.

Manavta Setu Foundation isi khaai ko bharne ka ek chhota sa prayas hai—jahan har sahayog seedha zarooratmand ke kaam aaye, bina kisi dikhawe ke, poori imandari aur samvedna ke saath.

Hamare Seva Siddhant

  • Har sahayog seedha zameeni zarooratmand tak pahunchane ka prayas
  • Bhojan, chhat, dawai aur aapda rahat ko sarvpratham prathmikta
  • Har samajik aur dharmik bhedbhav se upar uthkar sirf manavta ki seva
  • Volunteers aur sahayogiyon ke saath spasht aur imandar samvad
Seva Sahayog

Aapka Ek Chhota Sa Daan Kisi Ki Zindagi Badal Sakta Hai

Aap ₹51 se shuruat kar sakte hain ya apni ichha anusaar koi bhi rashi daan kar sakte hain—har yogdan seedha bhojan, aashray, baadh rahat aur bachchon ke ilaaj me lagaya jaata hai.

Aapka Sahayog Kahan Lagta Hai?

Seva ke liye bade daan ki nahi, sachche man ki zaroorat hoti hai. Aapke yogdan se hum yeh zameeni kaam aage badhate hain:

1. Annadaan & Ration Sahayog Bhookhe aur beghar logon ko garam bhojan aur zaroori ration pahunchana.
2. Bachchon Ke Ilaaj & Dawai Beemar bachchon ke doctor checkup, jaanch aur dawaiyon me turant madad.
3. Baadh & Aapda Rahat Sankat ke samay peedit parivaron ko chhat, kapde aur khane ki vyavastha.
